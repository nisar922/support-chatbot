ShopEasy Support FAQ

Return policy: You can return any product within 14 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

Shipping: Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 150 rupees extra. Orders above 999 rupees get free standard shipping.

Password reset: Click "Forgot password" on the login page, enter your registered email, and open the reset link we send you. The link is valid for 30 minutes.

Payment methods: We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery for orders up to 5000 rupees.

Support hours: Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST. You can email support@shopeasy.example.
